Reader diagnostics

Click the field and fire the reader at any code. Repeat two or three times.

#ReadLengthEnds withDurationPer key

What is measured. A reader types the whole code at a few milliseconds per character; a person takes a hundred or more. When the difference is clear, the Scan screen can fire on its own when it recognises a scan and stay out of the way when someone types by hand.

If "Ends with" says (nothing), the Scan screen won't recognise the scan: configure the reader to send Enter using the code sheet in its manual. If what was read doesn't match the label (a dash that comes out as an apostrophe, say), the reader is set to a different keyboard layout than Windows.
